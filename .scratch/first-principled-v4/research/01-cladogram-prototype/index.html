Cladogram + one-shot grow (ticket 01)

Locked v1 ticket 17 geometry: crown at the top, central trunk, layers hanging left and right (even right, odd left, deepest nearest the trunk per side). One-shot elapsed grow on load (~1s), then freeze at full visibility. Opacity is never bound to scroll.

On load the trunk draws, then layers bud deepest-first (physics first, apps last). When the timeline ends every layer stays at opacity 1. Scrolling the page or the stage must not hide or fade anything. Reduced motion (OS or the checkbox) skips grow and sap and shows the full Tree instantly. Horizontal scroll of the stage is the 375px fit: the document itself must not overflow.
