## Question

A Reality Map is a DAG: extra parents, side nodes, a declared trunk.
The Etymonline example is a fan-in at the top, then a mostly linear
spine. How does Dependence map onto that chrome without becoming a
timeline copy of Chronology?

## What

HITL. Lock the graph-to-picture rule.

Working recommendation: one downward spine is the trunk walk. Extra
parents merge in from the side the way `ex` and `ducere` merge into
`educere` - fan-in, not a second hanging column of cards. Fan-out stays
rare; invitation/rabbit-hole is fog. Arrange still chooses the
Dependence edges; this ticket only chooses how those edges are drawn.
Kill a picture that is just Chronology regimes in date order with
arrows.

## Answer

One downward spine is the trunk walk of Dependence, not Chronology
order. Extra parents merge in from the side (fan-in), the way `ex` and
`ducere` merge into `educere`. No second hanging column of cards.
Fan-out and rabbit-hole invitations stay fog. Arrange still picks the
edges; this ticket only picks how they are drawn.
