Phylogenetic motion prototype (ticket 07)

The portfoolio RootTree motion ported onto our zero-dep SVG concept tree: sap pulses rising from foundations to the crown, scroll-driven growth, node lifecycle animation, reduced-motion respect. Plain ES modules + SVG + CSS, no React.

Scroll to grow the tree: the trunk draws down, then each layer buds in from the deepest foundation up to the crown. Sap pulses ride the trunk and branches upward via SMIL animateMotion. Toggle each effect or simulate reduced motion to compare. The OS status line reports the real prefers-reduced-motion setting; when it is reduce, the JS gate skips sap and scroll wiring entirely.
