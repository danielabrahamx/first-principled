THE ONE THING MOST LIKELY TO GO WRONG. Not the seam. It is tempting to
write an adapter that is forgiving, because 14 of 20 words fail the gate
and a forgiving adapter would make the numbers look better. That is
exactly the fitted-to-the-fixture mistake this repo has already made
once, on MIN_NODES. The adapter's job is to assemble a selection the
generator accepted. Given a selection selectTopology returned ok:false
for, it must fail honestly and return the reason. It must NOT drop the
offending edges, must NOT reach into droppedJudgments for replacements,
and must NOT reinterpret the selection. Salvaging a rejected selection is
the v7 pattern. The gold rate is 0 to 2 of 4 across eight runs; an
adapter that lifts it by inventing structure has cost more than it
measured.

THE SECOND THING, AND IT IS NOT OBVIOUS. The v9 generator produces NO

FOUR QUESTIONS, IN THIS ORDER. The ticket lists them in full.
1. What is a layer? selectTopology returns ranks, computed as leaves 0
   and every other node one above its deepest prerequisite. The
   RealityMap wants ordered contiguous layers. Is rank the layer, or can
   a rank gap appear?
2. Does layer contiguity hold? validateRealityMap rejects any layer with
   no edge to a strictly lower layer, and MAX_REALITY_LAYERS is 12.
   Ranks are computed over the selected nodes so it should hold by
   construction, but prove it on a real run. A failure here is silent
   until the frontend draws it.
3. What does honesty look like with no history? See above.
4. What EdgeType does a Dependence edge get? Pair judgments are
   dependence only; EdgeType allows six. depends-on for all of them is the
   only defensible answer, but state it as a decision rather than leaving
   it implicit in the code.
